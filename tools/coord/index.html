--- layout: default title: Coordinate Converter permalink: /tools/coord/ description: Convert horizontal (azimuth/altitude) coordinates to equatorial (RA/Dec) for your location and the current time. lead: Convert azimuth/altitude to right ascension and declination for your site and the current time. eyebrow: Tools ---

Enter your observing location and a direction (azimuth/altitude) to get the current equatorial coordinates. Azimuth is measured from North, increasing toward the East. Because Right Ascension depends on time, the result updates every second.
