Research Experiences for Teachers

Digital Signal Processing in Radio Astronomy 2019

Digital Signal Processing in Radio Astronomy (DSPIRA) is an NSF Research Experiences for Teachers (RET) in Engineering and Computer Science Site at the West Virginia University Lane Department of Computer Science and Engineering. A brief description of the 2019 program.

The Cohort

This program was attended by a group of six excellent highly motivated teachers from all over the country. The participants included:

  • John Makous, NC
  • Howard Chun, RI
  • Tad Herman, NY
  • John Clarke, FL
  • Robert Baker, CA
  • Michael Stover, WV
